**Fig. 2 | Segmented Xenium transcripts resolve the cell types and niches of a lung cancer section in space.**
**a**, H&E of the section (Xenium Human Multi-Tissue and Cancer panel; 10x Genomics dataset<sup>1</sup>), cropped to the Xenium image. Lines, sopa tissue pieces (saturation mode), keyed by whole area; bold, the analysed piece (153,067 of 162,254 cells); frame, a 300 µm region. **b**, H&E of the region framed in **a**. **c**, 53,184 transcripts in the region (panel genes, quality ≥ 20; grey); four marker genes in color, enlarged. **d**, Each stain scaled from zero to its 99.5th percentile in the region (18S RNA not shown); frame, a 60 µm zoom. **e**, Zoom framed in **d**, stains as in **d**, cells segmented by Xenium Onboard Analysis 2.0 (33% from the membrane stain, 65% from interior stains, 1.5% by nucleus expansion). **f**, DAPI of the zoom, shown as in **d**, with nuclei segmented from it. **g**, Segmentation quality of the analysed piece's 153,067 cells (nucleus area / cell area: 147,600 with a nucleus); last bins include cells above 300 transcripts (134) or 300 µm² (263). Light grey, 5,844 cells below 10 transcripts, excluded hereafter. **h**, UMAP of the remaining 147,223 cells by Leiden cluster (resolution 1; 30 principal components of median-normalized, log-transformed counts). **i**, Marker genes per cluster (78–25,036 cells): color, mean normalized expression, min–max scaled per gene; dot area, fraction of cells with a transcript. Bars, each cluster's cell type. **j**, Cell types at 60% opacity over DAPI shown as in **d**; grey, cells excluded in **g**. **k**, Per cell, normalized expression of the higher of *EPCAM* and *LTBP2* (bold in **i**; *LTBP2* on ties), scaled 0–3 (region maximum 2.79); outlined only, cells expressing neither. **l**, CellCharter niches (30 principal components with their means over three layers of Delaunay neighbours; Gaussian mixture, six components set), drawn as the union of their cells' polygons, closed by 2 µm (containing 99.98% of their cells' centroids). Left, niches at 45% opacity over cells colored as in **j** at 30%. Right, per niche (n = 22,986/25,913/29,391/14,043/33,696/21,194 cells; 529/1,174/2,801/1,950/3,823/1,731 polygons; niches 1–6): cell-type composition; area-weighted mean area and roundness (4π area / perimeter²) of its polygons; median cell-centroid distance to the piece's outline. Scale bars, 1 mm (**a**), 50 µm (**b**–**d**, **j**–**l**) and 10 µm (**e**, **f**). FFPE, formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded; H&E, hematoxylin and eosin; DAPI, 4′,6-diamidino-2-phenylindole; αSMA, α-smooth muscle actin; UMAP, uniform manifold approximation and projection.
